# console

## Description

The Monkey Trading web app, to be served at console.monkey.trading: the client for the open-source CFD
engine in `monkey-trading/engine`, whose deterministic core runs inside a TEE. One full-view trading
terminal: watchlist, chart, order ticket, account panels, and a Funding page, with the access rules
(geo-block, eligibility declaration) and a check of the node's attestation before it signs. It runs
against a mock node in the browser that mirrors the engine node's REST and stream shapes, or, in a
build that pins a node, against a live client that verifies the node's attestation and every answer
it signs, behind one `EngineClient` interface. This file is an index: load only the rule whose hint
matches the current action.

## Structure

- **src/app/** — the App Router: `layout.tsx` (fonts through `next/font`, the `metadata` and
  `viewport` exports, and the robots meta tag), `robots.ts` (disallow all) with its test, `restricted/page.tsx` (the static page the edge geo-block rewrites a restricted country's requests to), `notices/page.tsx` (the static privacy and third-party notices — including the stored wallet connection choice, session key, trading preferences and exit proof — which the risk line and the WalletConnect dialog link to), `(console)/` (the route group whose layout holds the provider and shell, and the trading, House, MONKEY, Funding and Settings screens), `globals.css` (Tailwind
  CSS 4 `@theme` tokens shared with the website's design language, and the reduced-motion rule),
  and the icons.
- **src/app/(console)/house/** — the House screen, linked from the
  top bar's page links (Trade, House, MONKEY, Staking, Funding, Settings), as a section of three
  tabs (monkey-trading/console#311): `layout.tsx` renders `HouseSection`
  (`src/components/house/HouseSection.tsx`), which holds the heading, the Overview, Risk and
  Withdrawals tab bar and the house reads,
  opened once per visit to the section (`openHousePanel`, `houseStats.setVisible`), so the tab the
  route opens finds them running; each card's own feeds follow it only while its tab is mounted.
  `page.tsx` (`HouseOverview`, the default tab) holds the House figures (the house's cash, NAV,
  unrealised result, queued withdrawals, gross exposure and volume), open positions with each
  market's long, short and net, MONKEY's supply, stake and staker rewards, linking to the MONKEY and
  Staking pages, exposure against the cap, the parameters and the checkpoints, and the link to the
  history of NAV, exposure and the queue on the Dashboard; `risk/page.tsx` (`HouseRisk`) the
  close-out map, the position map, the house result at ±1% and the market caps; `withdrawals/page.tsx`
  (`HouseWithdrawals`) the withdrawal queue's totals, the connected account's place and the
  withdrawal health. Every figure is from an
  answer the node's enclave signed (generated and unsigned on the mock build).
- **src/app/(console)/monkey/** — the MONKEY screen, linked from the top bar
  between House and Staking, as a section of two tabs (monkey-trading/console#311): `layout.tsx`
  renders `MonkeySection` (`src/components/monkey/MonkeySection.tsx`), which holds the heading, the
  Emissions and Activity tab bar and the one client panel both tabs read through (`MonkeyPanel`,
  opened for the page it serves: `PanelPage` in `panel-state.ts`), created once per visit to the
  section, so what it holds survives a tab switch; the panel's reads start once the node is trusted
  and stop when the section is left. `page.tsx` (`MonkeyEmissions`, the default tab) holds the
  account's MONKEY emissions with their claim and the token, and the link to the history of
  emissions and supply on the Dashboard; `activity/page.tsx` (`MonkeyActivity`) the latest
  activity. It reads only while on screen: the
  node's `/v1/monkey` totals and activity about every 30 seconds,
  the wallet's chain reads one call at a time, bounded in time and never asking the wallet to do anything,
  on load, every 15 seconds, after a transaction and on an event for the account, and each read again straight away, at most once a second, on a stream event that changes it
  (a stake, an unstake, a delegation, an election, an epoch, a distribution, an emission, a deposit, a claim, a key, a checkpoint: `live-reads.ts`). There is no Refresh button and no Live switch anywhere in the console; the timers run only while the page is on screen and the tab visible, a failing read backs off (5 s doubling to 2 min) and says so calmly on its panel, and a claim panel draws no verdict until its reads have landed and offers a claim on the node's word that its proof is claimable (`claimable`, as `checkpointOf` in `engine/claim-standing.ts` reads it; the vault's latest seq, read through the wallet, decides only for a node that omits the field), so a new checkpoint never takes an offered claim away, and a vault refusal on send shows in plain words (`engine/claim-refusal.ts`). While the node holds no proof for an account in an epoch that paid stakers or a closed period it lost in, a claim card says what is known and never that nothing was distributed (`pending.ts`: the epoch, what it paid stakers as the closed epochs state it, and an estimated share from that; the node's allocated totals count the operator's fifth too, so the page labels them as the whole distribution), and the page reads the proofs every 5 s and on each signed checkpoint frame (`client.watch`; `rereadsFor` sees commands only) until one exists. A node without the routes answers 404 and its panels say so calmly. While a card's first reads run, and before the page load has settled its wallet (`walletDecided`), the card holds its loaded layout with dashes, its controls closed and a button's place kept unseen, so nothing below it moves when a read lands (monkey-trading/console#286). The MONKEY page's wallet read is the vault's record of the account's MONKEY claims, whose stall or failure the emissions card states and the backoff counts; the Staking page's the stake and the node's distribution proof; each page reads the activity and the wallet figures only it shows.

## Repo facts

- Default branch `main`; issues and milestones live in GitHub Issues on
  `monkey-trading/console`. The engine the app is a client of is `monkey-trading/engine`, whose
  README and decision issues settle every behaviour the app shows.
- **Merging needs the owner's go-ahead.** Branch rules require `ci-passed` and `merge-approved`, and
  allow squash merges only. Only after the owner approves a PR in chat does the session they told
  post `merge-approved` and merge it, and only as
  [agent-create-pr](skills/agent-create-pr/SKILL.md#merging) describes.
- **A merge is a production deploy.** Once the repository is connected to Vercel, every merge to
  `main` deploys production and every other pushed branch gets a preview deployment. Agents never
  change Vercel settings, environment variables, domains, or DNS, and never trigger a deploy,
  without the owner's explicit approval for that action ([no-deploy](rules/no-deploy.md)).
- **Statically rendered, with no backend of its own.** Every route prerenders at build time; nothing
  may make a route dynamic or add a route handler (`route.ts`), server action, or proxy
  (`proxy.ts`, or the deprecated `middleware.ts`) without the owner's approval. The app talks to
  the engine node from the browser only.
- **The mock follows the engine where it models it,** and keeps one known simplification for the
  owner to settle: a deposit before a declaration, which it refuses where a node holds it pending
  until a declaration credits it (its wallet deposits through the form alone, and the declaration comes first there). Its wallet holds a fixed 50,000 USDC less its deposits.
  It holds its demo account's session key from load, since its node starts afresh on every load and nothing
  of it persists; any other connect goes through setup or the key prompt as a live one does.
- **Restricted regions are blocked at the edge.** A `beforeFiles` rewrite in `next.config.ts`,
  built from `src/lib/geo-block.ts`, sends requests from the listed countries to the static
  `/restricted` page; an unknown country is allowed, and the engine node's refusal stays the
  enforcement. The list is a placeholder (the US and its territories) until counsel supplies it;
  changing it is a reviewed change. The whole console is `noindex` by the owner's decision: the
  root layout's robots meta tag, an `X-Robots-Tag` header in `next.config.ts`, and `robots.ts`. Blocked visitors run the root layout but not the `(console)` layout, so the root layout stays free of the provider, wallet and
  engine code.
- **The engine is the source of truth.** Every number, rule, and message the app shows about the
  engine matches the engine's README and its decision issues.
- **Private by construction.** No analytics, cookies, browser storage, trackers, third-party
  requests, or embeds without the owner's approval; fonts are bundled at build time. Four approved
  exceptions, all disclosed on /notices. The wallet connection choice (monkey-trading/console#82) — the
  connector kind and, for WalletConnect, the session topic — persists in `localStorage` across page
  loads, cleared when the connection ends (Disconnect, the wallet app ending the session, or a failed
  reconnect). The session key (monkey-trading/console#168) — its private key and address, the account,
  chain and vault it is scoped to, and its expiry — persists there until it expires, is revoked, the node
  stops accepting it, or the wallet disconnects, whether or not the wallet signs a revocation; it can
  trade for at most 24 hours and can never move funds, and it never enters the snapshot, a message or a
  log. The trading preferences and the last size placed in each market (monkey-trading/console#169) persist
  there too. The connected account's latest exit proof (monkey-trading/console#36) persists there per chain,
  vault and account, sent only through the wallet to its chain provider to be checked, until a later proof replaces
  it, the node shows the account holds nothing, or the vault refuses it. The engine
  node's origin is the one cross-origin destination the app exists for, approved in
  monkey-trading/engine#292 (rule 6): the build's pinned origin and, by the owner's decision in
  monkey-trading/console#37, each timelock-approved candidate's registered hostname, and no other, are in the CSP
  `connect-src` with their `https:` and `wss:` schemes, named one by one, and every answer from a node is shown only if
  the attested enclave signed it for that request. WalletConnect's relay (`wss://relay.walletconnect.org`) is
  the one other, approved in monkey-trading/console#7, with its Verify service's frame
  (`https://verify.walletconnect.org`, approved by the owner as monkey-trading/console#27 records), and only in
  a build with a WalletConnect project id;
  chain reads go through the user's wallet, never to an RPC of the console's own. `/notices` states what leaves
  the browser.
- **The rules come from the website.** The checklist and the implementation skill were ported from
  the website repository. A module they name that this repository lacks (`content.ts`, `prng.ts`,
  `format.ts`, `components/motion.tsx`, `replay.ts`) is the path its convention takes once the
  first feature needs it, and their simulation, shell, and replay-demo items apply to the mock
  engine and any demo the console builds.
- **Next.js 16.** Its APIs differ from older versions. The version-matched docs in
  `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` are the reference
  ([web-implementation](skills/web-implementation/SKILL.md#framework-reference)).
